WINWDM native x86 fully specified line interrupts on QEMU 10.0.13, PC, TCG, one Pentium CPU.
Device: QEMU EDU PCI 1234:11e8 at 05.0 on bus zero. INTx was enabled. No message-signaled interrupt was requested.
Probe SYS SHA-256 310e635ee515b83e825977896f510d3a21f491836f8e1b8cf774b5a097db8fc1

One unmodified probe binary ran against four different NT kernels. Before the IRQ tests it performed the
4,096-byte DMA write/read-back and verified all bytes. It registered the extended interrupt interface,
raised device mask 1, observed the ISR acknowledgement and a cleared status register, then disconnected.
A second registration raised and acknowledged mask 2 through the same device. Calls for automatic MSI
and an empty CPU affinity mask were rejected before connecting the interrupt.

The native NT 3.1 HAL lacks PCI enumeration and translated firmware IRQ 10 via its PC interrupt controller.
NT 3.50 and NT 4.0 reported the same line through the PCI HAL. NT 3.51 rewrote the IRQ line to 0x14 when
reading the device through HalGetBusData. HalGetInterruptVector on PCIBus translated that line to vector 0x51.
In an earlier NT 3.51 control, translating firmware IRQ 10 as ISA produced vector zero and the probe declined
the interrupt without attaching an ISR. The other three HALs used vector 0x3a.
