Windows 98 First Edition 4.10.1998, native WDM USB, QEMU 10.0.13.
Booted the user-supplied First Edition disk copy on the i440FX PC with its PIIX3 USB 1.1 host controller.
The WINWDM 6.0 kit VxD had loaded and passed its own PCI diagnostic before either USB hub was hot-plugged.
Microsoft USB.INF bound the host, root hub and external hubs through the operating system’s original NTKERN.
