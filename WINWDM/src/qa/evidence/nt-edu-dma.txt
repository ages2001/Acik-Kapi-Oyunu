Common-buffer DMA round trip on Microsoft x86 Windows NT and QEMU 10.0.13 TCG.
Devices: QEMU EDU 1234:11e8, default 28-bit DMA mask, 64 MiB guest, one Pentium.
The identical native WKEDUDMA.SYS used the WINWDM SDK, the guest HAL and its bus-visible address.
One 8192-byte common buffer supplied two disjoint 4096-byte regions. The driver initialized
the destination with the complement of the expected data, copied the source to the device,
cleared the source on the CPU, read the device buffer into the destination, compared every
byte, restored the PCI command register, and released the allocation.
