IDENT v1.3 - ICH5 PATA boot fixes (issue #4)
Windows NT 3.51 SP5 | Release notes | 19 November 2026

The reporter confirmed that NT 3.51 boots from the primary PATA SSD on
the ANOVO AIMB-865 in Enhanced Mode and sees all disks. The same build
also booted on the ASUS P8B75-M with no apparent regression.

AIMB-865 boot failure and fix
----------------------------
IDENT found the primary PATA channel at 0x1F0. The NT hardware
abstraction layer could not map its PCI bus-master registers at 0xFC00
through the EISA bus. IDENT fell back to PIO but still reported the
unmapped range to ScsiPort. The PCI scan ended with configuration error
0xC0040003. IDENT had already marked the primary channel as claimed,
so the ISA scan skipped the boot SSD. Only the secondary channel at
0x170 started, leaving NT to stop with INACCESSIBLE_BOOT_DEVICE (0x7B).

IDENT now checks the bus-master mapping before registering the adapter.
If the mapping fails, it registers the usable command ports, control
port and IRQ on the ISA bus and operates the channel in PIO. PCI
enumeration can then continue to the other PATA and SATA channels.
IDENT marks the ICH5 compatibility channel as claimed when ScsiPort
calls HwInitialize, allowing the ISA scan to retry it if registration
fails before that point.

Related changes included in v1.3
--------------------------------
- Channel detection honors the firmware's enable and routing settings
  in ICH5 Enhanced and Combined modes. Native IDE ports are relocated
  only when their addresses overlap another IDE channel. This also
  addresses a port allocation problem on the B75.
- After an ICH5 PATA software reset, IDENT retries empty or floating
  status for up to 150 ms. A ready disk proceeds immediately.
- Before polling a new ATA command or PIO phase, the NT driver reads
  alternate status and waits one microsecond. This prevents a stale
  status from making IDENTIFY appear complete before the data is ready.
- On ICH5 PATA, the host's cable indication determines whether modes
  above UDMA 2 are allowed. A 40-conductor indication caps the channel
  at UDMA 2. A usable bus-master mapping permits DMA when the drive and
  cable support it.
- bin/NT/diagnostic/ident.sys shows PCI resources, IRQs, disk detection
  and transfer modes on the NT boot screen. bin/NT/ident.sys is the
  regular driver.

Verification
------------
In QEMU 10.0.13 with NT 3.51 SP5, denying the EISA mapping for
0xFC00-0xFC0F reproduced the earlier driver's PCI error and STOP 0x7B.
With the same failure, the updated driver reached logon from the primary
PATA disk and enumerated both native SATA channels. With the mapping
available, the primary disk ran at UDMA 5. The physical board report
confirms boot and disk visibility. Its transfer mode and throughput
were not reported.
